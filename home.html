FAQ

Common questions

How do I transfer my data to a new phone?

Because Steady never uploads your health history to a cloud server, transfers stay under your control. Open Settings → Data & Reset, export a JSON backup file, and import it on your new phone or browser in seconds.

Does it work on iPhone and Android without Telegram?

Yes. Steady works directly in Safari and Chrome as a lightweight web app (and can be added to your Home Screen like a native app). Telegram Mini App is completely optional.

Which medications are supported? Can I pause a course?

Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, Saxenda, Rybelsus, plus custom medications with any weekly or daily schedule and custom dosage step. You can also pause and resume a course anytime after a doctor visit.

Is Steady a medical device?

No. Steady is a personal self-tracking journal and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or calculate medication doses, and the medication-level curve is an illustrative estimate only. Always follow your doctor's prescription.
